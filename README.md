## 📖 About

Beyond Mars is a futuristic idle tap game where players gather resources, upgrade technology, establish colonies, and expand humanity beyond Mars.

Designed for the **LevelUp Game Hackathon**, the game combines simple gameplay with strategic progression.

---

## 🎮 Gameplay

- Tap to mine resources
- Earn Energy & Minerals
- Unlock new buildings
- Upgrade mining efficiency
- Expand your Mars colony
- Complete missions
- Unlock advanced planets
